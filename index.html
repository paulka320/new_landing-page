About ATMOS

Professional embedded sensing for real spaces.

ATMOS combines a compact ESP32-S3 and An Arduino Mega boards with a curated suite of sensors and relay outputs. It senses climate, air quality, light, sound , then acts on that data through dependable firmware rules and a polished Flutter dashboard.

The project is built for environments where responsiveness matters: decision-making at the edge, room-level context, and a clear presenatation layer for operators.

About the project

ATMOS is a climate and environment control system that turns sensor data into real-world outputs, combining monitoring and automation in one professional package.

01 — Core controller

ESP32-S3

The project brain — Wi-Fi, firmware logic, and relay orchestration run locally so ATMOS stays responsive even when the app disconnects.

What it does
  • Dual-core Xtensa LX7 running firmware and Wi-Fi side by side
  • Evaluates every sensor rule locally — no cloud round-trip
  • Keeps relays and automations running if the dashboard disconnects
02 — Climate sensing

DHT11

Temperature and humidity readings feed climate-aware rules — trigger fans, adjust relays, and keep indoor comfort within safe bounds.

What it does
  • Single-wire digital read for temperature and relative humidity
  • Feeds climate rules that trigger fans and vents automatically
  • Sampled every few seconds — enough for room-scale comfort control
03 — Air quality

PMS5003

Laser particle sensing tracks PM2.5 levels in real time, enabling ventilation and filtration responses before air quality becomes a problem.

What it does
  • Laser-scatter particle counter reading PM1.0, PM2.5 and PM10
  • Flags unsafe air quality before it becomes noticeable
  • Triggers ventilation and filtration relays automatically
04 — Ambient light

BH1750

Precision lux measurement drives adaptive lighting — dim when daylight is sufficient, brighten when rooms need it.

What it does
  • I2C ambient light sensor with a direct lux readout — no calibration math
  • Wide 1–65,535 lux range, dim rooms to direct sun
  • Drives adaptive lighting: dim when daylight is enough, brighten when it isn't
05 — Acoustic sensing

INMP441

High-fidelity I2S audio capture for noise monitoring, voice triggers, and security awareness without dedicated analog modules.

What it does
  • I2S digital MEMS microphone — 24-bit audio, no noisy analog stage
  • Tracks ambient noise levels for comfort and awareness
  • Can trigger sound-based automations without a dedicated audio board
06 — Occupancy

PIR motion

Passive infrared detection triggers occupancy-based automations — lights, fans, and security modes that respond to presence, not schedules.

What it does
  • Detects infrared changes from moving bodies in its field of view
  • Purely passive — zero engagement needed from occupants
  • Wakes occupancy-based automations: lights, fans, security modes
07 — Manual control

Capacitive touch

A touch-sensitive input gives operators a local manual override at the panel itself, no app required.

What it does
  • Detects a touch via capacitance change — no moving parts to wear out
  • Gives a local manual override right at the panel
  • Immune to the dust and moisture that degrade mechanical buttons
08 — On-device display

SSD1306 OLED

A compact onboard display shows live readings directly on the unit — no phone or app needed to check room status.

What it does
  • 128×64 monochrome I2C display, sharp at close range
  • Shows live readings directly on the unit — no app required
  • Low enough power draw to stay on continuously
09 — Full assembly

Integrated system

Every sensor, display, relay channel, and firmware rule comes together in one compact build — extendable by design as new inputs and outputs are added.

System summary
  • 8 sensor & display modules feeding one firmware loop
  • 1 relay channel switching real hardware
  • Every layer above, in one compact, extendable build
